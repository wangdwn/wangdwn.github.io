Faster Than a Blink: How Pro Gamers Train Their Reflexes

职业选手零点几秒的反应，是天赋还是练出来的？答案藏在训练室，也藏在一门叫「运动科学」的课里。

In a professional esports match, a round can be decided in less than half a second. A player spots a moving target, aims, and fires—all before most people finish blinking. That speed looks like pure talent, but it rests on a foundation of deliberate practice and physical training.

在一场职业电竞比赛里，一局可能在不到半秒内分出胜负。选手发现移动目标、瞄准、开火——这一切都在大多数人还没眨完眼之前完成。那种速度看起来像纯天赋，其实它扎根在刻意的练习和身体训练之上。

Studies on competitive gamers show top players react in about 180 milliseconds on average, while ordinary people take roughly 250. The gap sounds small in daily life, but in a game it is the difference between winning and losing.

对竞技玩家做的研究显示，顶尖选手平均反应时间约 180 毫秒，普通人则要大约 250 毫秒。这个差距放在日常生活里听起来很小，但在游戏里，它就是胜负之别。

Training reflexes is not just about playing more. Professional players break their skills into small parts—aim, movement, decision—and drill each part again and again. They also train their bodies: steady sleep, exercise, and proper meals keep the brain's signals sharp.

训练反应速度，不只是多打几盘那么简单。职业选手把技能拆成小块——瞄准、走位、决策——然后一遍遍地反复打磨。他们也会训练身体：稳定的睡眠、锻炼和合理的饮食，让大脑的信号保持敏锐。

Focus matters as much as speed. In a long tournament, a player must stay concentrated for hours. Distractions are costly. Many teams hire coaches who design routines to protect attention, much like trainers do for athletes in traditional sports.

专注和速度一样重要。在一场漫长的锦标赛里，选手必须一连几小时保持集中。分心代价高昂。很多战队会请教练设计日常流程来守护注意力，就像传统体育里教练为运动员做的那样。

Esports is also a team sport. Five players must communicate, share information, and trust each other's decisions. A split-second call can turn a losing fight into a victory. That is why teams practice coordination as much as individual skill.

电竞同样是一项团队运动。五名选手必须沟通、共享信息，并信任彼此的决定。一个瞬间的呼叫，能把一场要输的团战变成胜利。这就是为什么战队既练个人技术，也练配合。

Of course, gaming carries risks. Sitting for long hours and staring at screens can harm posture and eyes. Smart players balance training with breaks, outdoor exercise, and real-world friendships—the same habits that protect any kind of performance.

当然，打游戏也有风险。长时间久坐、盯着屏幕，会伤到姿势和眼睛。聪明的选手会用休息、户外运动和现实中的友谊来平衡训练——这些习惯对任何领域的表现都有保护作用。

So the next time you watch a tournament, remember: the reflexes on screen are not magic. They come from practice, health, and focus—a lesson that applies far beyond the game.

所以下次看比赛时，记住：屏幕上的反应不是魔法。它们来自练习、健康和专注——这个道理，远不止适用于游戏。
