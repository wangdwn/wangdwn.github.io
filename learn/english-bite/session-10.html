The Mind of an Octopus

一只章鱼，五亿个神经元，三分之二在八条手臂里。它不是我们的近亲，却独立进化出了聪明的大脑——科学家说，它可能是我们最接近「外星智慧」的生物。

外媒原文 · 节选（Scientific American）

Octopuses and their relatives (cuttlefish and squid) represent an island of mental complexity in the sea of invertebrate animals. Our most recent common ancestor is so distant—more than twice as ancient as the first dinosaurs—that they represent an entirely independent experiment in the evolution of large brains and complex behavior. They are probably the closest we will come to meeting an intelligent alien.

A common octopus has about 500 million neurons in its body. Human beings have many more—something nearing 100 billion—but the octopus is in the same range as various mammals, close to the range of dogs. In an octopus, the majority of neurons are in the arms themselves—nearly twice as many in total as in the central brain.

— "The Mind of an Octopus", Peter Godfrey-Smith, Scientific American, 2017

The octopus is a strange kind of genius. It has no bones, and it can change its color and shape in a second. But what really surprises scientists is its mind.

章鱼是一种奇怪的「天才」。它没有骨头，能在一秒内改变颜色和形状。但真正让科学家惊讶的，是它的心智。

An octopus has about 500 million nerve cells, or neurons. That may sound small next to the 100 billion neurons in a human brain, but it is close to the number in a dog's brain. And here is the strange part: most of an octopus's neurons are not in its brain at all. Nearly two-thirds of them live in its eight arms. Each arm can feel and even taste on its own.

一只章鱼大约有 5 亿个神经细胞，也就是神经元。跟人脑的 1000 亿个相比，这听起来很少，但它接近狗的大脑数量。最奇怪的地方在于：章鱼大部分的神经元根本不在大脑里，近三分之二分布在它的八条手臂上。每条手臂都能自己感受，甚至自己「品尝」。

Octopuses are also clever problem-solvers. In aquariums, they have learned to open jars to get food, and some have even turned off lights by shooting water at the bulbs. They can find their way through simple mazes and remember the best route to a reward.

章鱼也是聪明的解题高手。在水族馆里，它们学会了打开罐子取食，有的甚至朝灯泡喷水来关灯。它们能穿过简单的迷宫，并记住通往奖励的最佳路线。

Why does all this matter? Because the octopus is not our close relative. Our shared ancestor lived long before the dinosaurs. Yet the octopus built a complex brain and smart behavior on its own, along a completely different path from ours. If we ever want to imagine what an intelligent alien might be like, the octopus may be the closest thing we can meet.

这些为什么重要？因为章鱼并不是我们的近亲。我们共同的祖先生活在恐龙出现之前很久。然而，章鱼沿着一条与人类完全不同的路，独立演化出了复杂的大脑和聪明的行为。如果我们想想象智慧外星人长什么样，章鱼也许是我们能遇到的最接近的存在。
