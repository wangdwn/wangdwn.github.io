Session 03 · 生物 · 高二

Earth's Quiet Tough Guys

缓步动物：微观却极端耐造——真空、干涸、辐射都难轻易「劝退」。

~328 词 ~7 分钟 高二 深度 ★★★☆☆ 难度 ★★★☆☆ 朱砂 高考常考
本篇怎么读

  2. 先通读英文抓大意；卡壳再点蓝色/朱砂词。
  3. 释义里若出现「词根馆」链接，可跳去巩固同一词根。
  4. 读完做配对、填空、用法、限时回忆与推断题（约两分钟）。

If you imagine the toughest animal on Earth, you might picture a shark or a polar bear. Biologists often point somewhere smaller: a tardigrade, a creature so tiny that it looks like a walking water bear under a microscope.

如果让你想象地球上最能扛的动物，你也许会想到鲨鱼或北极熊。生物学家常常指向更小的地方：缓步动物，小到在显微镜下看起来像一只会走路的水熊。

Tardigrades live in moss, soil, and the thin films of water on leaves. When conditions turn harsh, many species do not simply die. They enter a state that looks almost like death—shutting down metabolism so deeply that they can survive extreme dryness, freezing temperatures, and even the vacuum of space in carefully designed experiments.

缓步动物住在苔藓、土壤和叶片上薄薄的水膜里。环境变恶劣时，许多种类并不直接死去。它们进入一种几乎像死亡的状态——把新陈代谢压得极低，从而在精心设计的实验里熬过极端干燥、冰冻，甚至太空真空。

The key idea is not magic armor. It is a survival strategy. Some proteins help protect cell structures; water is replaced in ways that prevent ice crystals from tearing membranes. When moisture returns, the animal can revive and continue its quiet life.

关键不是什么魔法盔甲，而是一套生存策略。一些蛋白质帮忙保护细胞结构；水分被替换掉，以免冰晶撕开细胞膜。潮气回来之后，它又能醒过来，继续自己安静的生活。

For high-school readers who like biology, tardigrades are a reminder that “adapt” is more than a textbook verb. Adaptation can mean changing what a body does when the environment refuses to stay friendly. It can also mean waiting—pausing life processes until the world becomes livable again.

对喜欢生物的高中生来说，缓步动物提醒我们：“适应”不只是课本上的一个动词。适应可以是环境不肯友好时，身体改变自己的做法；也可以是等待——把生命过程暂停，直到世界重新宜居。

Scientists study these creatures because extreme survival raises useful questions. How do cells keep DNA from breaking under stress? Which molecules act like shields? Answers may someday help preserve medicines, crops, or even human tissues, though popular headlines often exaggerate how close we are to “immortal astronauts.”

科学家研究它们，是因为极端生存会引出有用的问题。细胞怎样让 DNA 在压力下不断裂？哪些分子像盾牌？答案也许有一天能帮我们保存药品、作物，甚至人体组织，尽管流行标题常把“永生宇航员”说得太近。

Still, the lesson for a curious student is clear. Nature’s toughest solutions are often microscopic. Before you dismiss a puddle of moss as boring, remember that it may hide an animal that has practiced patience for hundreds of millions of years—and that patience, in biology, can look like strength.

对好奇的学生，教训很清楚：自然界最能扛的办法，常常是微观的。别急着把一洼苔藓当成无趣，它里面也许藏着一只练习了数亿年耐心的动物——而在生物学里，耐心看起来可以像力量。

When you next open a biology diagram of cells and membranes, ask a harder question: what happens when the diagram’s polite conditions disappear? Tardigrades answer with a shrug of chemistry and time. They do not conquer the desert; they wait it out, then walk again.

下次打开细胞和细胞膜的生物图，问一个更难的问题：图上那些客气的条件消失之后会怎样？缓步动物用化学和时间耸耸肩作答。它们并不征服沙漠，它们把沙漠等过去，然后继续走。
