Session 07 · 物理·导航 · 高考冲刺

Clocks That Need Einstein

手机地图为什么准？原子钟 + 相对论修正——导航里的「高考物理彩蛋」。

~308 词 ~8 分钟 高考冲刺 深度 ★★★★☆ 难度 ★★★★☆ 朱砂 高考常考

Your map app feels ordinary until it fails in a tunnel. Behind the blue dot is a constellation of satellites carrying atomic clocks. Receivers on Earth compare timing signals and calculate position. Tiny errors in time become large errors in distance, because light—and radio signals—travel so fast that a billionth of a second already maps to roughly thirty centimeters.

地图应用在隧道里失效之前，看起来平平无奇。蓝点背后，是一群携带原子钟的卫星。地面接收机比较时间信号，算出位置。时间上的微小误差会变成距离上的大误差，因为光和无线电信号快到一纳秒大约对应三十厘米。

Here is the surprise that delights physics teachers: without corrections from relativity, GPS would drift unacceptably. Clocks in orbit experience both special-relativistic effects from their speed and general-relativistic effects from weaker gravity. Engineers do not debate philosophy in the control room; they apply formulas so that the system remains accurate for navigation, aviation, and surveying.

让物理老师高兴的惊喜在这里：没有相对论修正，GPS 会漂到不能用。轨道上的钟既受速度带来的狭义相对论效应影响，也受较弱引力带来的广义相对论效应影响。工程师不在控制室里辩论哲学，他们套公式，让系统对导航、航空和测绘保持准确。

You do not need to derive Einstein’s field equations to use a phone. You do need the habit of asking what assumptions hide inside a “simple” tool. A map is not only geography; it is synchronized time distributed across space. When someone says science is useless outside exams, GPS is a polite counterexample in your pocket.

用手机不必推导爱因斯坦场方程。你需要的是一种习惯：问一个“简单”工具里面藏着什么假设。地图不只是地理，它是分布在空间里的同步时间。当有人说科学出了考场就没用，GPS 就是口袋里一个客气的反例。

For exam readers, the passage trains a familiar skill: follow a chain from phenomenon to principle to consequence. Phenomenon: precise positioning. Principle: time and gravity couple to measurement. Consequence: technology must include corrections or accuracy collapses. That chain mirrors how good reading items are built—and how lab reasoning is graded.

对考试读者，这篇文章训练的是熟悉的能力：顺着一条链，从现象到原理，再到结果。现象是精确定位；原理是时间和引力共同参与测量；结果是技术必须带上修正，否则精度崩掉。这条链既像好的阅读题怎么出，也像实验推理怎么判分。

Critics sometimes claim relativity is “only theory.” Operational systems answer differently: if the corrections were omitted, errors would accumulate far beyond what drivers and pilots can ignore. Theory earns trust when predictions survive daily use at planetary scale.

有人批评说相对论“只是理论”。运行中的系统给出不同的回答：如果去掉这些修正，误差会累积到司机和飞行员无法忽略。当预言在行星尺度上经受住每天的使用，理论才赢得信任。

So the next time the blue dot snaps to the right street, thank an invisible chorus of clocks—and a century-old insight that time is not a background drumbeat the same for everyone. Navigation works because physics refused to stay on the chalkboard.

所以下一次蓝点准确地落到那条街上，谢谢一组合唱般看不见的钟，也谢谢一个有百年历史的洞察：时间不是对所有人都一样的背景鼓点。导航能用，是因为物理拒绝只待在黑板上。
