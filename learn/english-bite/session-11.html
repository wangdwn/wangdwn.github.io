Potatoes in Space

如果人类将来在月球建家，我们吃什么？答案可能是最不起眼的土豆——科学家刚刚在假月壤里把它种了出来。

外媒原文 · 节选（The Guardian）

The humble potato could be the key to sustaining human life on the moon, according to a new scientific report. Prolonged lunar exploration, it says, will depend on life-support systems able to produce food there.

Potatoes are an ideal candidate for farming on the moon, scientists on a Nasa-backed project concluded, after they grew them in simulated lunar soil. "Potatoes provide more calories per land area than many other crops," said Oregon State University plant biologist Aymeric Goyer, who led the study. "They are nutritious—they provide more potassium than a banana, are rich in vitamin C, have good proteins—and they have a high satiety index."

— "Spuds in space: scientists say potatoes could be key food source for future moon base", The Guardian, 2026-11-19

If humans ever build a home on the moon, what will we eat? The answer might be a humble potato. Scientists have just grown potatoes in fake moon soil—and it worked.

如果人类将来在月球建家，我们吃什么？答案可能是最不起眼的土豆。科学家刚刚在假月壤里种出了土豆——而且成功了。

A potato is a great space food. It gives more calories than many other crops, and it is full of good things: more potassium than a banana, plenty of vitamin C, and useful protein. It also keeps you full for a long time.

土豆是很棒的太空食物。它比很多作物提供的热量更多，而且营养丰富：钾比香蕉多、维生素C充足、还有有用的蛋白质。它还能让你长时间不饿。

But moon soil has a problem—it has no nitrogen, so plants cannot grow in it alone. The researchers mixed the moon soil with worm compost, soil made by worms breaking down food waste. With just 5% worm compost, the potatoes grew roots. With 30%, they grew as well as potatoes in normal Earth soil.

但月壤有个问题——它没有氮，所以植物没法单独在它里面生长。研究人员把月壤和蚯蚓堆肥——也就是蚯蚓分解食物垃圾做成的土壤——混合起来。只要加 5% 的蚯蚓堆肥，土豆就能长根。加到 30% 时，土豆长得和普通地球土壤里一样好。

Why does this matter? Both the US and China plan to build long-term bases near the moon's south pole. Nasa hopes to return astronauts to the moon in 2028, and China aims to do the same before 2030. If people are going to stay there, they will need to grow their own food.

这为什么重要？美国和中国都计划在月球南极附近建立长期基地。NASA 希望在 2028 年把宇航员送回月球，中国的目标则是在 2030 年前。如果人们要长期待在那里，就需要自己种食物。

So the next time you look up at the moon, remember: one day, the first food grown there might be a potato.

所以下次抬头看月亮时，记住：有一天，那里种出的第一种食物，可能就是一颗土豆。
